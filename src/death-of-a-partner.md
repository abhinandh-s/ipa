# Death of a Partner

### Sec. 33

### Sec. 34

When a partner is adjudicated as an Insolvent.

- he cease to be the partner on the same date of adjudication
- whether or not the firm is thereby dissolved 

### Sec. 35

If the firm is not dissolved by the death of a partner as per the underlying contract.

Then the estate of a deceased partner **is not liable for any act Firm done after his death**.
