### Sec. 9 - General Duties of partners

Partners are bound-
- to carry on the business of the firm to the greatest common advantage;	
- to be just and faithful to each other; and	
- to render true accounts and full information of all things affecting the firm, to any partner or his legal representative.	
- Fraudulent partner(s) must indemnify the firm for any loss caused such act in the conduct of the business of the firm.

### Sec. 11(1) - Rights and duties of partners

The mutual rights and duties of the partners of a firm may be determined by thr Partnership deed. Such contract may be expressed or may be implied by a course of dealing.

Change in contract - only by consent(expressed or implied) of all partners.

### Agreements in restraint of trade

| Section | When it applies | Conditions |
|:---:|:----|:-----|

| 36(2) | on ceasing to be a partner | he will not compete with the firm for a specified period or within specified local limits |
| 54 | upon or anticipation of the dissolution of the firm | some or all partners will not compete with the firm for a specified period or within specified local limits |

### Sec. 12 - Conduct of business [Default, can be overriden by Partnership Deed]

| Rights/Obligations | Description |
|:----|:----|
| Participation | every partner has right to take part in the conduct of the business |
| Diligence |  must attend diligently to his duties |
| Decisions | - ordinary matters of business = majority partners[^majority] <br>change in nature of business = all partners |
| **access** to and to **inspect** and **copy** any of the books of the firm | every partner/heirs or legal representatives or their duly authorised agents of deceased partner have right to do so |

[^majority]: may be decided by a majority of the partners, and every partner shall have the right to express his opinion before the matter is decided

> [!Court Ruling]
> - The Act gives right to every partner to take part in the conduct of the business, only if Partnership deed doesn't contain any counter provisions
> - Transferees have no business rights under Section 29
> - a partner is not liable for negligence if he can show that he used such skill and intelligence as he possessed in the conduct of the business.
